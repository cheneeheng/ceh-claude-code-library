# Ideas Backlog

Every idea for this repository: a new plugin, skill, agent, hook, or validator check, or a change
to one that exists. An idea can come from anywhere: our own sessions, a user, or a competitor
analysis. The gstack, pstack, superpowers, ECC, and mattpocock/skills analyses of 2026-10-07
seeded the list with every competitor skill, agent, and workflow that our work covered only partly
or not at all, except what each analysis put under Skip. Where several sources do the same job, the
ideas are merged into one entry. Nothing here is committed work.

The list has two tables. **Done** holds every idea that was built, or confirmed as already covered
by what exists. **Open, on hold, and rejected** holds everything else: open, waiting on evidence,
deferred, on hold, dropped for now, and rejected. A rejected entry stays listed so it is not
proposed again (principle 9).

Every entry is checked against [`VISION.md`](VISION.md) before it is added, and a **Note** records
where the vision changes it. Hook and guard entries need a failure that guidance alone did not
prevent before they are built (principle 7). A new idea goes into the second table, and the PR that
builds it moves the row to Done.

Effort: S is under a day, M is a few days, L is longer. Source is the competitor component the idea
came from, or "own idea" with the date it was proposed.
